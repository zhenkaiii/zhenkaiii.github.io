Sub-8-Bit Quantization for On-Device ASR

Achieving extreme compression for speech recognition models through novel quantization-aware training techniques

Kai Zhen, Martin Radfar, Hieu Duy Nguyen, Grant P. Strimel, Nathan Susanj, Athanasios Mouchtaris
IEEE SLT 2022 & Interspeech 2022
← Back to Home

The Challenge

On-device automatic speech recognition (ASR) requires models that are both accurate and efficient. Traditional quantization methods force you to choose between:

❌ Fixed Centroids

Predetermined quantization levels that can't adapt during training, limiting compression potential.

❌ Heavy Regularization

Complex training procedures with multiple hyperparameters that are difficult to tune.

❌ Accuracy Loss

Significant degradation in word error rate (WER) when pushing below 8-bit quantization.

Our Solution: Two Complementary Approaches

1. S8BQAT: Lloyd-Max Inspired Quantization

Key Idea: Use Lloyd-Max compression theory with a Multi-Regional Absolute Cosine (MRACos) regularizer to push weights toward optimal quantization centroids.

💡 Innovation

The MRACos regularizer aggregates weights toward their nearest centroid during training, effectively acting as a pseudo-compressor without requiring complex hyperparameter tuning.

2. General Quantizer (GQ): Regularization-Free Approach

Key Idea: "Soft-to-hard" compression with self-adjustable centroids in μ-Law constrained space—no regularization needed.

💡 Innovation

Unlike traditional methods, GQ learns the quantization centroids during training. The μ-Law constraint ensures perceptually meaningful compression, inspired by audio coding principles.

Technical Deep Dive

The Connection to Audio Coding

Both approaches draw inspiration from classical signal compression:

  • S8BQAT uses Lloyd-Max quantization, a foundational technique in lossy compression
  • General Quantizer leverages μ-Law companding, widely used in telephony and audio codecs
  • Both recognize that model compression ≈ data compression: reduce bit depth while preserving perceptual quality

Hardware-Aware Design

The quantization schemes are designed with hardware accelerators in mind:

  • Sub-8-bit quantization enables efficient use of hierarchical GPU memory
  • Reduced precision allows more operations per clock cycle
  • Memory bandwidth becomes less of a bottleneck
